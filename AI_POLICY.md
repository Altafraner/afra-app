# AI Policy

Definitions and notations from [DEFINITIONS.md](DEFINITIONS.md) apply to this
document.

We recognize AI to be a powerful tool that might enable you to complete more
tasks faster. For that reason we welcome you to use AI as a tool in creating
your contributions.

However, whilst we recognize AI might be useful to you, we require you to be
responsible for your tool's actions. For that reason please follow these rules.
- You MAY use AI as a tool in your workflow for any contribution, including
  security reports.
- You MUST NOT send us untested AI-generated code.
- You SHOULD NOT send us AI-generated claims you have not verified yourself. If
  you do so you MUST state so clearly.
- You MUST NOT let the AI communicate in your stead. All communication MUST
  originate from yourself or another human adhering to this policy.
- You MUST NOT brush off any mistakes you made as the AIs fault.
