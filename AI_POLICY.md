# AI Policy

Definitions and notations from [DEFINITIONS.md](DEFINITIONS.md) are used in
this document.

We recognize AI to be a powerfull tool that might enable you to do more tasks
faster. For that reason we welcome you to use AI as a tool in creating your
contributions.

But whilst we recognize AI might be usefull to you, we require you to be
responsible for your tools actions. For that reason please follow these rules.
- You MAY use AI as a tool in you workflow for any contributions, including
  security reports.
- You MUST NOT send us untested AI-generated code.
- You SHOULD NOT send us AI-generated claims you have not verified yourself. If
  you do so you MUST state so clearly.
- You MUST NOT let the AI communicate in your stead. All communication MUST
  originate from yourself or any other human adhering to this policy.
- You MUST NOT brush of any mistakes you made as the AIs fault.
